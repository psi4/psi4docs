:term:`SOSCF_START <SOSCF_START (SCF)>`
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

      The earliest iteration on which second-order SCF may start. Both this and :term:`SOSCF_START_CONVERGENCE <SOSCF_START_CONVERGENCE (SCF)>` must be satisfied. Starting earlier than the default gives the first-order iterations less room to settle the occupation, which a second-order step may (true for OTR) hold fixed.

      * **Type**: integer
      * **Default**: 3
