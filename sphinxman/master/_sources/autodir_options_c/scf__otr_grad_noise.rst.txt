:term:`OTR_GRAD_NOISE <OTR_GRAD_NOISE (SCF)>`
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

      For :term:`SECOND_ORDER_ORBITAL_OPTIMIZER_PACKAGE <SECOND_ORDER_ORBITAL_OPTIMIZER_PACKAGE (GLOBALS)>` = `OTR`, whether to perturb the orbital gradient. The perturbation helps the solver leave a stationary point, which is wanted when seeking a minimum and not when a calculation means to sit at a saddle point, and it moves the converged orbitals within :term:`D_CONVERGENCE <D_CONVERGENCE (SCF)>` enough to show in a sensitive response property.

      * **Type**: :ref:`boolean <op_c_boolean>`
      * **Default**: true
