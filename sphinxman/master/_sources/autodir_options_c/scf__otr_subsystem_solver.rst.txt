:term:`OTR_SUBSYSTEM_SOLVER <OTR_SUBSYSTEM_SOLVER (SCF)>`
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

      For :term:`SECOND_ORDER_ORBITAL_OPTIMIZER_PACKAGE <SECOND_ORDER_ORBITAL_OPTIMIZER_PACKAGE (GLOBALS)>` = `OTR`, solver used for the trust-region subproblem. The Davidson family comes in a linear-system (`_LS`) and an augmented-Hessian (`_AH`) formulation; `_LS` is OpenTrustRegion's own default. `TCG` is often faster, markedly so with diffuse basis sets, but is less robust: it can stall before reaching the convergence threshold.

      * **Type**: string
      * **Default**: DAVIDSON\_LS
